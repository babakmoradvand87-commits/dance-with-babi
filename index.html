How to dance

  1. 1

    Take a dial each

    Hold one device with both hands: player one owns the left dial, player two the right one. On a keyboard, player one uses A/D and player two ←/→.

  2. 2

    Turn together

    The pearl only moves by the average of both dials, so nobody can carry the dance alone. Turn both dials the same way — clockwise rolls forward.

  3. 3

    Meet the rings on the beat

    Every golden ring is one beat of the waltz. Pass a ring exactly when it sounds: perfect, good, early, late. Keep a steady, gentle turn and the whole piece lines up.

  4. 4

    Keep the ribbon

    If you twist against each other the ribbon between you frays and your combo snaps. Stay together — that is the whole dance.

Headphones help. Volume is your friend, the music is your metronome.
