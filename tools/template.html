WS player one  ·  ↑↓ player two  ·  on touch: lift your half of the screen

How to dance

  1. 1

    One end each

    You are both holding the same plank. Player one holds the left end, player two the right one. On touch: lift a finger in your half of the screen. On a keyboard: WS and ↑↓.

  2. 2

    One up, one down

    The plank only tilts when the two of you disagree: your end up, your partner's end down. If you both lift together the plank stays level and the pearl does not move an inch — so no one can carry this dance alone.

  3. 3

    The slope is the speed

    Steeper plank, faster pearl — and it answers your hands a heartbeat late, so you have to lead. Start the swing before you need it and level the plank to stop it.

  4. 4

    Be in the ring on the beat

    Every ring is one beat of the waltz and they alternate between the two ends: one for you, one for your partner. It is judged the moment the drum lands — perfect, good, on the edge. Overshoot and the pearl slams into the end of the plank, and your streak snaps.

خلاصه به فارسی: هر بازیکن یک سر تخته را بالا می‌برد و پایین می‌آورد. اگر هر دو با هم بالا ببرید تخته صاف می‌ماند و مروارید حرکت نمی‌کند؛ پس باید یکی بالا و دیگری پایین باشد. شیب تخته سرعت مروارید است و مروارید با کمی تأخیر جواب می‌دهد، پس باید یک ضرب زودتر شروع کنید. هر حلقه یک ضرب والس است و باید مروارید دقیقاً روی ضرب داخل حلقه باشد.
